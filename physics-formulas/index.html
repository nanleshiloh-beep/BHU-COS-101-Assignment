Below are some formulas in physics:

  1. Newton's Second Law (Law of Acceleration): The acceleration of a body is directly proportional to the net force acting on it and inversely proportional to its mass. Mathematically, the law of acceleration is represented as F = ma
    Where; F = Force, M = Mass, A = acceleration.
  2. Speed (v) = D/T
    Where D = Distance and T = Time
  3. Ohm's Law: V = IR
    Where V = voltage passing through a conductor, I = Current, and R = Resistance.
  4. Pressure = F/A
    Where F = Force and A = Area.
  5. Kinetic Energy (K.E) = 1/2 mv²
    Where M = mass, and V = Velocity.
